# Inner seasons

When **Visual seasons** is on in Settings, the Calendar status card uses a seasonal scene — winter, spring, summer, or autumn — and a small badge with the season name.

This is a product metaphor (“inner seasons”). It is not a weather forecast and not a lab result.

## Opening the guide

Tap the season badge. The card expands until it fills the window:

- The seasonal artwork stays at the **top as a header** (cycle day, phase name, short line).
- The badge becomes a **close (X)** control. Tap it again, or press Escape, to collapse.
- Below the header there is an educational guide: what the season means, what it is often good for, what often helps, and what to ease up on.

If Visual seasons is off, the card uses the plain primary style. There is no badge, so there is no guide.

## How seasons map to phases

| Inner season | Cycle phase |
| --- | --- |
| Winter | Menstrual |
| Spring | Follicular |
| Summer | Ovulation (estimated window) |
| Autumn / Fall | Luteal |

If the phase is labelled **estimated**, the season is estimated too. See [Calendar phases and “estimated”](/help/calendar-phases-estimated).

## What the lists are for

The expanded panel is wellbeing copy: rest in winter, rising energy in spring, connection in summer, winding down in autumn. “Do” and “don’t” are **optional suggestions**, not rules.

They always include the product limits:

- Ciclo does **not** confirm ovulation.
- Inner seasons are **not** contraception and not a fertility test.
- Severe pain, very heavy bleeding, or symptoms that stop daily life deserve clinical care.
